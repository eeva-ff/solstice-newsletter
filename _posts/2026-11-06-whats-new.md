## ⛑️ Fixes and improvements

- Editing a text box on an animated banner no longer pushes the copy off the edge when you save, so it doesn’t look deleted anymore. Versions already saved that way still need a quick manual or chat fix. ([SOL-3927](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3927))
- Banner storyboards are back. Banners generated on 4 and 5 Nov were built without one. ([SOL-3949](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3949))
- Emails that switch layouts at 600px now show the desktop layout in the PRC desktop frame, on the guest review page and in PDF exports. ([SOL-3945](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3945))
- Clients on auto-approval brands no longer sit on “Your asset is being prepared”. The first version publishes straight away and the owner gets the asset-ready email. ([SOL-3901](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3901))
- Clients can upload a draft PDF from Edit content again. ([SOL-3922](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3922))

## 🔜 Coming soon

- **Brand setup (in testing):** build a brand’s rule book with an agent, surface by surface (colors, typography, ISI, layouts and more), then run a taste test of four banners and four emails from it before anything changes in generation.
- **Self-healing comments:** comments stay attached to the right spot as the asset changes. Still in progress.
- **MLR check:** a new editor tab that catches typos, doubled words and punctuation before MLR, with Fix with chat on each one.
- **Campaigns:** request several assets in one intake chat and see them together on a Campaigns tab in Home.

## ⚠️ Known issues, we’re on it!

- **PRC and Content staying in sync:** after a chat edit, the PRC Template and Content tabs can both show Content. Still being worked on.
- **Font availability:** PRC templates can still be finicky when a client’s brand font isn’t available, so RBAs and fonts can look different in exports.
