## ✨ New

### Chat edit: change the creative and the PRC in one request

One request can now change both the creative and the PRC. You review them together, with one before/after and a single accept or revert.

When the agent has a question for you, it stays in the chat, and you answer it in the message box.

### MP4s are real asset versions

Upload an MP4, play it in the canvas, and export it as an MP4 download or a view-only link.

### Rename a brand from Brand Profile

Anyone with access to a brand can now rename it from Brand Profile.

## ⛑️ Fixed

- Manual banner edits no longer break layout, spacing or animation, or drop copy from other scenes.
- Agency banner ZIPs with one ZIP per size now upload properly, and a ZIP that can’t be read is kept as a normal upload instead of failing.
- Pasted frame copy is used as written.
- Links to nested folders open the right folder (thanks Ciaran and Vanessa!).
- Renamed assets show up in search, and new owners get notified when an asset is handed to them.
