## 🚢 Shipped this week

**New**

- **Compatibility Checker:** run it from the editor's Actions menu and keep working while it checks the version on screen. Emails get a gallery of every client rendering with filters and an exportable report, and a compatible version is saved for you when the check passes. Banners get a checklist with renders, a list of what to fix and a downloadable package.

**Fixes and improvements**

- The chat editor remembers what it just proposed. Refresh, switch tabs or step away, and a short "yes" or "approve the second one" applies the right change. ([SOL-3881](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3881))
- Email previews in chat work again for emails that use brand images and fonts. ([SOL-3891](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3891))
- Switching between PRC Template and Content after a chat edit no longer shows the Content view on both tabs. ([SOL-3765](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3765))
- An asset shared with a client no longer looks changed the next morning. ([SOL-3867](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3867))
- When your session expires you land on the login screen and come back to the page you were on, instead of a blank page. ([SOL-3874](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3874))
- Dictation on the new request page can use your microphone again. ([SOL-3916](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3916))

## 🔜 Coming next

These are in production behind a flag and in testing now:

- **Pre-MLR check:** an MLR Check tab in the editor that catches typos, doubled words and punctuation before review, with Fix with chat on each finding. It can also run your client's regulatory checklist against the asset.
- **Campaigns:** start a campaign from intake with several assets, get a brief for each, and see all of a brand's campaigns in a new Campaigns tab on Home.
- **Resolve client comments in chat:** the chat editor pulls in the client's latest comments and applies them in one go.
- **Notifications you control:** notifications stay in the bell until you clear them, and every notification names the asset by its file name.
