## ✨ New

### Compatibility Checker: see every inbox before your client does

An email that looks right in the editor can still break in Outlook or on a phone, and until now the only way to find out was to send test emails and check them one by one.

The Compatibility Checker lives in the editor’s Actions menu:

- **Runs in the background:** it checks the version on screen, so you can keep working while it runs.
- **Emails:** a gallery of every client rendering, with filters and an exportable report. When the check passes, a compatible version is saved for you.
- **Banners:** a checklist with renders, a list of what to fix and a downloadable package.

## ⛑️ Fixes and improvements

- The chat editor remembers what it just proposed. Refresh, switch tabs, approve a change or step away for a while, and a short reply like “yes” or “approve the second one” now applies the right change. No need to repeat yourself. ([SOL-3881](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3881))
- Email edits with brand images and fonts work again. The chat editor can now check its own work on emails that use images from outside the design library or brand fonts (like the Pfizer ones), so it no longer gets stuck on those edits. ([SOL-3891](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3891))
- Assets were generating twice, so one you’d shared with a client could look changed the next morning. We fixed that. ([SOL-3867](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3867))
- An expired session now takes you to login and back to where you were, instead of a blank page. ([SOL-3874](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3874))

## 🔜 Coming soon

- **Self-healing comments (next Monday):** comments stay attached to the right spot as the asset changes. We’re fixing drift between PRC and Content first, which this depends on.
- **MLR check:** a new editor tab that catches typos, doubled words and punctuation before MLR, with Fix with chat on each one. It can also check against your brand’s own regulatory checklist, uploaded in brand setup.
- **Campaigns:** request several assets in one intake chat and have each one filed into your project with generation started, then see them together on a Campaigns tab in Home.
- **More accurate claims:** we’re redesigning the claims library so your assets pull in the right claims more reliably.

## ⚠️ Known issues, we’re on it!

- **PRC and Content staying in sync (fix next Monday):** after a chat edit, the PRC Template and Content tabs can both show Content. (Derrick Ogola)
- **Font availability:** PRC templates can still be finicky when a client’s brand font isn’t available, so RBAs and fonts can look different in exports. We’re making sure we can host your brand fonts on the platform.
