## Compatibility Checker: see every inbox before your client does

An email that looks right in the editor can still break in Outlook or on a phone, and until now the only way to find out was to send test emails and check them one by one.

The Compatibility Checker lives in the editor’s Actions menu and checks the version on screen in the background, so you can keep working while it runs. Emails get a gallery of every client rendering with filters and an exportable report, and when the check passes a compatible version is saved for you. Banners get a checklist with renders, a list of what to fix and a downloadable package.

## The chat editor remembers what it just proposed

Refresh the page, switch tabs or step away for a while, and the chat editor used to lose track of what it had offered, so you had to explain the change all over again.

Now a short reply like “yes” or “approve the second one” applies the right change, however long you were away.

## Fixes and improvements

- **Email previews in chat:** previews work again for emails that use brand images and fonts. ([SOL-3891](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3891))
- **PRC Template and Content tabs:** switching tabs after a chat edit no longer shows the Content view on both. ([SOL-3765](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3765))
- **Shared assets:** an asset shared with a client no longer looks changed the next morning. ([SOL-3867](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3867))
- **Expired sessions:** you land on the login screen and come back to the page you were on, instead of a blank page. ([SOL-3874](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3874))
- **Dictation:** the new request page can use your microphone again. ([SOL-3916](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3916))

## Coming next

These are in production behind a flag and in testing now.

- **Pre-MLR check:** an MLR Check tab in the editor that catches typos, doubled words and punctuation before review, with Fix with chat on each finding. It can also run your client’s regulatory checklist against the asset.
- **Campaigns:** start a campaign from intake with several assets, get a brief for each, and see all of a brand’s campaigns in a new Campaigns tab on Home.
- **Resolve client comments in chat:** the chat editor pulls in the client’s latest comments and applies them in one go.
- **Notifications you control:** notifications stay in the bell until you clear them, and every notification names the asset by its file name.
