## ✨ New

- **RBAs now support resizing and adjusting callout boxes**, plus diagonal lines. [Loom walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/c194806e73d54339a5c8373869cdcb03)
- ***Banner inspiration:*** generate four different concepts of the squarest banner size and pick the one you like from the thumbnail picker. Your pick becomes the next version, and the previous version keeps all four.
  - Turn on this feature in the brand metadata section (lmk if you need help!).
- Reviewers on a **review-only link can export it as PDF or HTML** with comments included.

## ⛑️ Fixes and improvements

- Reverted chat edits no longer come back on the next prompt. ([SOL-3752](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3752))
- Email review labels no longer get wiped when switching between manual and chat edits. Once a proof exists, subject line and preheader are hidden from the content view so they can't be overwritten. (Vanessa Buckmelter 🤞)
- Veeva search now finds documents by the end of their number, so 01540 returns MAT-US-ELA-01540. ([SOL-3749](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3749))
- Fewer failed chat turns.
