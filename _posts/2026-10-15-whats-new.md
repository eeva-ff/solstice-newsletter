## ✨ New

- **QC diff checker** is live! Compare two PRC versions and track every change, and hopefully not any unintentional ones. 🤞
- **Banner inspiration:** get several design options every time you create a banner, then pick the one you want to build on.
- Open assets in a new tab from a project folder with cmd/ctrl-click, middle click or right-click. (Ciaran!)
- ***Coming very very soon (in final testing):*** New intake flow: final testing in prod, then release.

## ⛑️ Fixes and improvements

- The 1/2/3 scene buttons on the Content board now follow the banner as it plays. ([SOL-3526](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3526))
- PDF exports now match the proof on screen, so callouts sit on the same words. ([SOL-3781](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3781))
- Saving in the editor no longer piles up extra copies of the animation script. Creatives that already have extras clean up on their next save. ([SOL-3508](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3508))
- ISI scrapes and prescribing information extraction no longer fail the way they did on 7 and 8 Oct. ([SOL-3776](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3776))
- Chat edits no longer stall after the agent has already made the change. ([SOL-3808](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3808))
