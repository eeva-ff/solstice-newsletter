## ✨ New

- **PDFs in chat:** the chat editor now reads attached PDFs. It can pull an image out of a PDF and drop it straight into your design.

## ⛑️ Fixes and improvements

- We fixed stale tabs! A tab left open for hours now saves like a fresh one, so no more "Failed To Update Content" or reloading and losing your work. (Vanessa Buckmelter)
- We've allowed changes to Veeva job codes using the chat editor. It'll warn you and ask for approval. ([SOL-3833](https://linear.app/solsticehealth/issue/SOL-3833))

## 🔜 Coming soon

- **Resolve client comments with the chat editor (in testing):** it can read the comments on the asset you're working on and resolve them on command.
- **Pre-MLR QC:** check ISI and grammar on an asset. We're also making ISI more accurate and specific to each asset, so the check knows exactly what that email or banner should carry.
- **Automated brand setup:** new brands start closer to your client's look from V1, with stronger email layouts, animated banners and the right colors and styles.

## 🔭 On the horizon

- **Email compatibility checks:** confirm exported HTML renders correctly for emails and banners.
- **Bulk create and edit:** create campaigns, multiple assets at once, and track them more granularly.
- **Memory:** we're exploring ways for Solstice to remember what matters to you, so you don't have to start from scratch every time. Early days, more soon.
- **Smarter chat editor:** we're working on it getting better at understanding what you mean, so you spend fewer prompts getting to the edit you want.
  - We're also digging into how you use it today, so tell us what slows you down!

## ⚠️ Known issues, we're on it!

- PRC templates can still be finicky with fonts.
  - This causes issues when you export: RBAs and fonts might look different if the fonts are not supported.
- PRC templates can have flaky subject line and headers.
