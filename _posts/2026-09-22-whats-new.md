## ✅ Live now

- You can hide every comment pin on a PRC, so you can read the creative underneath
- Commenting on a PDF no longer breaks partway through when the asset is a PDF (e.g. Phatom Paks)
- Publishing to a client works again. It was failing on a quality check - we removed it
- Claim extraction no longer fails on PDFs that contain a very large page

## 🔜 In progress / Coming soon

- ***Agentic platform.*** We're revamping how V1 is generated, and getting it bug-free before we ship to you. Will update on release date asap!
- **The MCP vs platform sync problem:** Editing through the MCP will update the PRC template correctly. We've refactored to fix the versioning issues
- **More PRC fixes:** callouts and cover edits will persist when you save. When you ask chat to change a cover field the review will show your actual change instead of the old version
- **QC/Diff checking:** Side by side view of two PRC templates with the actual changes highlighted. We've made progress but deprioritized this cycle to ship the agent platform and PRC template fixes. Will update on release date soon!

appreciate everyone's time and patience as we figure out the best way to ship a product you can rely on. if you have any questions/feedback, please let me know. thank you!
